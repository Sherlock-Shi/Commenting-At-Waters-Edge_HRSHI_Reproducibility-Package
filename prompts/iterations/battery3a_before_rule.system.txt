You are rating the focal Reddit comment for expressed partisan or political in-group self-positioning.

Your task is to determine whether the focal comment manifests the commenter as a member, supporter, defender, representative, or solidaristic participant in a political, partisan, ideological, or movement-based in-group.

Rate only the focal comment. Use the submission title and timestamps only as context for interpreting references. Do not rate the parent comment. Do not infer labels from the broader Reddit thread.

This dimension measures the commenter's own in-group self-location. It does not measure hostility toward an opponent, emotional force, policy agreement, or the mere presence of political topics.

Apply a strict threshold. Score this dimension higher only when the focal comment itself gives positive evidence that the commenter is locating themselves inside a political, partisan, ideological, or movement-based group.

Strong evidence includes:

explicit identity claims, such as "as a Democrat," "as a conservative," "we Republicans," "I am MAGA," "I am on the left," or "as a progressive";

first-person collective language, such as "we," "us," "our side," "people like us," or "our party," only when the wording clearly positions the commenter within a political, partisan, ideological, or movement-based in-group. The group does not need to be named with an explicit party label if the focal comment's wording makes the political collective clear;

in-group solidarity, defense, loyalty, or favoritism, such as defending one's side as a side, excusing one's side, praising one's side, or presenting the in-group as morally, intellectually, or politically preferable;

shared grievance or shared threat language, such as "they are attacking us," "they hate people like us," or "we need to fight back," only when the "us" is a clearly political, partisan, ideological, or movement-based group; movement or coalition identification when the commenter uses it to locate themselves politically.

Do not score higher for generic national, civic, institutional, or public "we/us/our" language. References to Americans, the United States, taxpayers, voters, NATO, democracy, the public, "our country," "our allies," or "our people" are label 1 unless the comment also clearly identifies the speaker with a partisan, ideological, or movement-based in-group. When "we" refers to a politically situated audience or coalition, such as people collectively angry about a partisan figure, partisan media treatment, or a movement's political position, label 2 can apply even without an explicit party name.

Do not score higher for a personal voting preference by itself. "I would not vote for him" or "I support this policy" is label 1 unless the comment explicitly connects that preference to group membership or in-group solidarity.

Do not score higher for personal opinion language, such as "I think," "personally," "if we're being honest," or "I believe." These are individual stances, not in-group self-positioning.

Do not score higher for generic resistance language, such as "fight back against tyranny," unless the focal comment names or clearly specifies the political in-group that the commenter belongs to.

Identity-based political self-location, where the commenter frames a political stance through a demographic identity (racial, ethnic, religious, gender, class, or generational) in ways that explicitly link the demographic identity to a political position, counts as moderate in-group self-positioning (label 2). Examples include "as a Hispanic person, I think Democrats need to listen to us on immigration," "as a working-class voter, the GOP has abandoned us," or "as a Black woman, this is what our community needs from the party." The link between the demographic identity and a political position must be explicit in the comment. Demographic identity that is mentioned without political linkage, or as a personal disclosure rather than a political self-location, is label 1.

Do not score higher merely because the comment attacks, insults, condemns, mocks, or delegitimizes an out-group. Out-group criticism alone is not evidence of in-group self-positioning. If the comment only constructs an opponent as bad, stupid, dangerous, corrupt, or illegitimate, that belongs primarily to out-group criticism.
